THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Biography

The Neighbourhood, often stylized as The NBHD, is an American alternative rock band formed in Newbury Park, California, in 2011. The group consists of frontman Jesse Rutherford, guitarists Zach Abels and Jeremy Freedman, bassist Mikey Margott, and drummer Brandon Fried. From the beginning, the band was characterized by a consistent aesthetic vision in which black-and-white photography and dark, atmospheric music videos played a central role. Musically, they skillfully sit at the intersection of indie rock, pop, R&B, and hip-hop, giving their sound a very melancholic and modern edge. They celebrated their international breakthrough in 2013 with their debut single "Sweater Weather," which became a massive global hit and later experienced a massive, long-lasting revival through platforms like TikTok. Their accompanying debut album, I Love You., established them firmly in the global music scene. In the following years, the band released further acclaimed albums such as Wiped Out! in 2015—which features fan favorite "Daddy Issues"—as well as their self-titled album in 2018 and the conceptual work Chip Chrome & The Mono-Tones in 2020. With their distinct style and genre-bending experimentation, they have significantly shaped the modern alternative music scene for over a decade.

Discography

I Love You.(2013)

Their debut album features a mix of alternative rock and indie pop. It includes the famous song "Sweater Weather" and explores themes of love, relationships and loneliness.

Wiped Out!(2013)

This album has a dreamy and emotional sound. Its songs explore heartbreak, growing up and complicated relationships. Popular tracks include "Daddy Issues" and "The Beach".

Hard to Imagine The Neighbourhood Ever Changing (2018)

This album combines alternative rock, pop and electronic sounds. It shows the band's musical development and includes songs like "Scary Love", "Nervous" and "Softcore".

Chip Chrome & The Mono-Tones (2020)

This album introduces a new artistic style with a futuristic atmosphere. It tells a creative story through its songs and features tracks like "Pretty Boy" and "Cherry Flavoured".

(((((ultraSOUND))))) (2025)

The band's fifth studio album brings a fresh chapter to their musical journey. It combines their alternative sound with new influences and explores themes of identity, emotions and personal experiences.
